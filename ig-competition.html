IG Competition

The Interest Group in Competition (IGComp) is an informal research platform at the ZEW – Leibniz Centre for European Economic Research, Mannheim. IGComp promotes academic and policy discussions on topics broadly related to competition economics.

The group runs a regular internal workshop (open to interested researchers) where members and invited speakers present research at any stage of development. Occasional sessions address policy initiatives relevant to competition authorities and policymakers.
